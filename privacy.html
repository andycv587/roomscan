RoomScan / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

RoomScan is an iPhone app from VIS Lab that scans a room with the device's camera and LiDAR Scanner and turns it into an editable 3D floor plan. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Summary

RoomScan does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Camera and LiDAR

RoomScan asks for camera access because Apple's RoomPlan framework needs the camera and LiDAR Scanner to measure walls, doors, windows, and furniture. The camera feed and depth data are processed on your device only. They are never recorded, uploaded, or sent to us or anyone else.

Scans and exported files

Room scans stay on your device while you review and edit them. When you export a scan (USDZ, JSON, or DXF), the file is saved only to the location you choose in the Files app. RoomScan does not upload scans or exported files anywhere.

Data collection

  • No personal information, contacts, location, or identifiers are collected.
  • No usage analytics or crash data are sent to us.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

RoomScan is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
