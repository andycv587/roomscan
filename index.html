RoomScan

An iPhone app from VIS Lab

RoomScan guides you through scanning a room with your iPhone's LiDAR Scanner, then lets you review and edit the 3D floor plan and export it as USDZ, JSON, or AutoCAD DXF.

Requirements

  • An iPhone with a LiDAR Scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro models)
  • iOS 17.6 or later

How to use

  1. Tap Start Scanning and slowly move around the room.
  2. Tap Done to finish the scan and review the 3D model.
  3. Edit walls, doors, windows, and objects, then tap Done.
  4. Choose export formats and tap Export to save the files.
  5. Tap New Scan to start another room.

Formats

ArcGIS Pro can import USDZ directly. For ARA 3D Studio, convert the USDZ file to GLB or glTF with an open-source tool such as Blender.
